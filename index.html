Predictive Safety Risk Center

Safety Risk Forecasting Model

The Predictive Safety Risk Index computes real-time exposure scores (from 0.0 to 10.0) based on three safety metrics: Frequency Factor (number of incidents), Recency Factor (days elapsed since last incident, decaying over a 12-month curve), and Severity Factor (hazard level of the incident types, e.g. Recordable, Lost Workday, First Aid). This identifies high-risk jobsites, crafts, and supervisors so you can proactively schedule safety stand-downs, tool audits, or JSA compliance inspections before the next incident occurs.

Driving Questions Questionnaire

Identify contributing factors by answering standard checklist questions in key compliance categories.

Documentation (JSA & Permits)

Was the JSA complete, accurate, and signed by all crew members?
Were relevant safe work procedures (SWPs) and permits reviewed and followed?

Tools & Equipment (including PPE)

Was the equipment and tools in good working order?
Were tools and PPE used as intended and appropriate for the task?

Supervision & Oversight

Was supervision adequate and present during critical tasks?
Were unsafe acts or conditions addressed immediately by supervisors?

Knowledge & Training

Was the employee trained, qualified, and experienced in the task?
Were all hazard communications and safe procedures understood?

Procedural Compliance

Were established procedures and policies followed without deviation?
Was minimum PPE strictly used and enforced?

Shortcuts & Pressure

Were safety measures bypassed to meet production/schedule targets?
Did the employee feel undue pressure to complete the task quickly?

Environmental Conditions

Did weather, lighting, or surface conditions contribute to the incident?
Were environmental hazards uncontrollable or unforeseen?
